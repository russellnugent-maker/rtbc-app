How it works

From entry to the ninth stamp

  1. Register

    Enter once online. Your numbered digital race passport is issued the moment your payment goes through.

  2. Choose your races

    Any officially timed 21.1 or 42.2 km road race in each province. You choose the event and enter it directly with the organiser.

  3. Run and upload

    Finish the race, then upload your official result. Each province counts once it is verified.

  4. Collect your stamps

    Each province’s stamp unlocks in your passport once your result is verified. Nine stamps make you a finisher, and finishers receive a printed passport with all nine races filled in.

Race entries, travel and accommodation are booked and paid for separately by each runner. Qualifying races are organised independently and are not affiliated with Energy Lab.

The race passport

On your phone for the journey, in print when you finish

Your passport lives in the Run the Beloved Country web app, with your number, dates and stamps. Finish all nine provinces and we send you the printed edition: a forest-green booklet with your nine races, dates and times filled in.

The stamp set

Nine provinces, nine stamps

Each stamp is styled like a weathered ink travel stamp, with its own colour and a landmark from that province. Tap a province or a stamp.

Nine stamps make a finisher

Once all nine provinces are verified, your passport gets its finisher stamp and the completion page: I ran the beloved country.

Questions

Good to know

Which races count?

Any officially timed road half marathon (21.1 km), marathon (42.2 km) or road ultra held in the province, organised by any club or organiser. You choose the races. Trail races, virtual races and shorter distances don’t count.

Can I mix half marathons and marathons?

Yes. Any mix of 21.1 and 42.2 km races counts, in any order.

How long do I have?

24 months from the date your passport is issued.

Does my entry include race entries or travel?

No. You enter each race directly with its organiser and arrange your own travel and accommodation. Your challenge entry covers your digital passport, result verification and, when you finish, your printed finisher passport.

How are results verified?

You add your official race result in the app, with a results link or finisher certificate. Once it is checked against the official results, usually within 7 days, that province’s stamp unlocks.

Is the race passport a real passport?

No. It is an Energy Lab running challenge collectible, not an official travel or identity document.
