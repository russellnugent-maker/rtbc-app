1. About the challenge

1.1 Run the Beloved Country (“the Challenge”) is an independent, self-guided running challenge presented by Energy Lab Athletic Company, an unregistered business with its correspondence address at 21 Aloe Ridge, Wag ’n Bietjie Dr, Woodland Hills, Bloemfontein (“Energy Lab”, “we”, “us”).

1.2 By entering the Challenge you (“the Entrant”) agree to these terms, which form a contract between you and Energy Lab. Please read them, especially the clauses in bold, which limit our responsibility or place risk on you.

1.3 The Challenge is not a race. Energy Lab does not organise, stage or time any of the races you run as part of it.

2. Entry and eligibility

2.1 The Challenge is open to runners aged 18 or older, and to runners aged 16 or 17 with the consent of a parent or guardian, who must accept these terms on their behalf. We do not accept entries from anyone under 16.

2.2 You must meet each qualifying race’s own entry requirements, including its minimum age, licence, medical and cut-off rules. Entering the Challenge does not give you entry into any race.

2.3 Energy Lab membership is not required. You need a free account, which you create on this website when you enter. The same sign-in also works for the Energy Lab app and YNM (Your Next Move).

2.4 Each entry is personal to one Entrant and one passport.

3. Challenge rules

3.1 Goal. To complete the Challenge you must finish one qualifying race in each of South Africa’s nine provinces within your Challenge Window.

3.2 Qualifying race. An officially timed road race of 21.1 km (half marathon), 42.2 km (marathon) or longer (road ultra-marathon), organised by any race organiser and run on or after the date your passport is issued. Trail races, virtual races, time trials and races shorter than 21.1 km do not qualify. A race counts for the province it is listed under on RunningCalendar.co.za, as shown in the race finder. A race not listed there counts for the province of its official start venue. Races outside South Africa do not qualify.

3.3 Challenge Window. 24 months from the date your passport is issued, as shown in your passport. You may extend your Challenge Window once, by up to six months. The extension is free if injury or illness stops you finishing in time, supported by a note from a medical practitioner; otherwise it costs R250.

3.4 Verification. For each province you must complete the proof form on the website or in the Running Passport app (race, date, distance and finish time) and provide proof of your finish, being a link to the official race results or an official finisher certificate, within 30 days of the race. Results recorded only on a GPS watch or app are not accepted. Each province counts once, and our decision on whether a result qualifies is final, subject to clause 12. We check each submission against the race listing and your proof, partly with automated checks. Submissions that match are approved automatically; any that do not are reviewed by a person, and no result is rejected without a person reviewing it. We aim to complete checks within 7 days.

3.5 Stamps. Your passport is digital. Each province stamp appears in it once that province is verified, and your finisher stamp appears when all nine are verified.

3.6 Fair play. Running for someone else, submitting false results or altering a result will lead to disqualification from the Challenge without a refund.

3.7 Cancelled or changed races. If a race is cancelled, or shortened by its organiser for any reason, it does not count, and you will need to run another qualifying race in that province.

4. Fees, payment, cancellation and transfers

4.1 Entry fee. The entry fee is R499, including delivery of your printed finisher passport within South Africa. All prices are in rand. Energy Lab is not registered for VAT, so no VAT is charged. R50 of every entry fee is donated to our charity partner, the Binah Legacy Center.

4.2 What the fee covers. Your numbered digital race passport, result verification for all nine provinces, use of the Running Passport app and, if you finish, your printed finisher passport. It does not cover race entry fees, travel, accommodation, licences or anything you need to take part in a race.

4.3 Payment. Payment is made online through PayFast. Your entry is confirmed, and your passport issued, as soon as PayFast confirms your payment.

4.4 Cooling-off. You may cancel your entry without giving a reason within 7 days of entering, under section 44 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act. Email support@energylabapp.co.za and we will refund the entry fee in full within 30 days. Once you have submitted a result for verification, you have asked us to start providing the service and the cooling-off right no longer applies.

4.5 After the cooling-off period. Entry fees are not refundable once the cooling-off period has passed, except where the law requires otherwise. Entries are personal and cannot be transferred to another person.

4.6 If we cancel the Challenge. If Energy Lab cancels the Challenge before you have completed it, we will refund the full entry fee.

4.7 Partner offers. Entrants may be offered partner benefits, such as a discount on a YNM training-plan subscription bought on our website. These are optional extras with their own terms, are not part of the entry fee, and may change.

5. Printed finisher passport

5.1 When all nine provinces are verified, we print your finisher passport with your name, passport number and races, and send it by Pargo to the South African pickup point you choose. We aim to dispatch it within 15 working days of receiving your delivery details.

5.2 Risk in the printed passport passes to you when it is delivered to your pickup point. If it does not arrive within 20 working days of dispatch, tell us and we will trace or replace it.

5.3 A replacement printed passport can be ordered for R150, including delivery.

5.4 The passport, digital or printed, is a running collectible. It is not an official travel or identity document and may not be used or presented as one.
