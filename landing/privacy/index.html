Privacy Policy

Version 2 · Last updated 23 November 2026

1. Who we are

This policy explains how Energy Lab Athletic Company, an unregistered business (“Energy Lab”, “we”), collects and uses personal information for Run the Beloved Country (“the Challenge”). It covers the website runthebelovedcountry.co.za, the register-your-interest form, Challenge entries, and the Running Passport app for iPhone and Android.

Responsible party: Energy Lab Athletic Company, 21 Aloe Ridge, Wag ’n Bietjie Dr, Woodland Hills, Bloemfontein (correspondence address).

Information Officer: Russell Nugent, support@energylabapp.co.za.

2. Your account

You sign in to Run the Beloved Country with an Energy Lab account. The same account (your email address and sign-in) also works for the Energy Lab app and YNM (Your Next Move), which are also run by Energy Lab. If you already have an Energy Lab or YNM account, you can use it here.

We do not ask for your ID number, and we do not collect health information.

The app on your phone. The Running Passport app uses your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo or proof. It keeps a copy of your passport and stamps on your phone so you can open it without signal; signing out or deleting the app removes that copy. The app has no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Meta Pixel on our website. Our website (not the Running Passport app) uses the Meta Pixel, a small piece of code from Meta Platforms. It tells Meta which of our pages you visit and whether you start or complete an entry, and sets cookies in your browser. Meta may link this to your Facebook or Instagram account if you have one. We use it only to see which adverts bring people to the Challenge and to show our adverts to people likely to be interested; we do not send Meta your name, email or results. You can block it by declining or deleting cookies in your browser, and you can control how Meta uses this information in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings.

Automated checking of results. When you submit proof of a finish, our software, which uses an AI service, compares your proof with the race listing and the details you entered. Results that match are approved automatically. Any result that does not match is reviewed by a person: we never reject a result, or make any other decision about your entry, by automated means alone. You can ask for any decision to be reviewed by emailing support@energylabapp.co.za.

Direct marketing. We email you about Run the Beloved Country only if you have consented, or if you are an entrant and the message is about the Challenge. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, and you can opt out at any time. We will not sell your information or give it to sponsors or partners for their own marketing.

5. How long we keep it

  • Interest sign-ups: until you unsubscribe, or 12 months after the Challenge launches if you don’t enter.
  • Uploaded proof of finish (certificates, results screenshots): until your Challenge window ends, plus 6 months for any queries.
  • Entry and result records: 3 years after your Challenge window ends, so we can confirm finishers and deal with any claims.
  • Payment and accounting records: 5 years, as tax law requires.
  • Your name on the public finisher wall: only for as long as you consent.

After that we delete or anonymise the information.

6. Deleting your account — Running Passport

You can delete your account at any time in the app or on the website (Account → Delete account), or by emailing support@energylabapp.co.za. You have 7 days to change your mind and restore it; after that it is deleted permanently, together with your passport, results and photos.

Because the account is shared, deleting it also removes your Energy Lab and YNM sign-in. If you have a YNM subscription, cancel it first where you pay for it. We keep payment records for the 5 years tax law requires, with your personal details removed from them.

7. How we protect it

We use reputable hosting providers, encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls so only authorised people see entrant data, and we collect only what we need. If we become aware of a security compromise affecting your information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

8. Your rights, including deleting some of your data

You have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct or delete information that is wrong, out of date or no longer needed, without deleting your whole account (for example a photo, a result or your finisher-wall entry);
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time, including unsubscribing from emails (this doesn’t affect processing done before you withdrew);
  • lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

To use these rights, email support@energylabapp.co.za. We will respond within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days.

9. Children

Entrants aged 16 or 17 may take part only with the consent of a parent or guardian, who accepts the terms on their behalf. We collect the parent or guardian’s name and email to record that consent. A minor appears on the public finisher wall only if their parent or guardian opts in. We do not accept entries from anyone under 16.

10. Complaints to the Information Regulator

The Information Regulator (South Africa), Woodmead North Office Park, 54 Maxwell Drive, Woodmead, Johannesburg, 2191. Website: inforegulator.org.za. Enquiries: enquiries@inforegulator.org.za. POPIA complaints are lodged through the Regulator’s eService Portal on its website.

11. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use your information, we will email you first.
