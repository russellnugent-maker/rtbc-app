The Rules

In plain language · The full legal wording is in the Terms & Conditions

The challenge

Run one qualifying race in each of South Africa’s nine provinces within 24 months of getting your passport. Each verified race earns that province’s stamp in your passport. Collect all nine and you’re a finisher.

What counts as a qualifying race

  • An officially timed road race of 21.1 km (half marathon), 42.2 km (marathon) or a road ultra. Any organiser.
  • Run on or after the day your passport is issued. Earlier races don’t count.
  • Trail races, virtual races, time trials and anything shorter than 21.1 km don’t count.
  • A race counts for the province RunningCalendar.co.za lists it under. If it isn’t listed, the province of the start venue counts.
  • If a race is cancelled or shortened, it doesn’t count, and you’ll need another race in that province.

Proving your finish

  • Submit each race on the website or in the Running Passport app within 30 days of running it: race, date, distance and finish time.
  • Add a link to the official results or upload your official finisher certificate. GPS watch or app recordings alone aren’t accepted.
  • We check every submission, partly automatically. Ones that match are approved straight away; anything that doesn’t match is checked by a person. No result is rejected without a person looking at it. We aim to finish checks within 7 days.
  • Each province counts once.

Your 24 months

  • Your Challenge Window starts the day your passport is issued and runs for 24 months.
  • Entering before 1 December 2026? Your passport is dated 1 December 2026, so your 24 months, and the races that count, start then.
  • You can extend once, by up to six months: free if injury or illness held you up (with a doctor’s note), otherwise R250.

Entry

  • R499, of which R50 goes to the Binah Legacy Center. Your passport is issued as soon as payment is confirmed.
  • Open to runners 18 and older, and 16–17-year-olds with a parent or guardian’s consent.
  • Entries are personal and can’t be transferred. You can cancel within 7 days of entering for a full refund, as long as you haven’t submitted a result yet.
  • Your entry doesn’t include race entries, travel or accommodation.

Finishers

When all nine provinces are verified, your finisher stamp appears and we print your finisher passport, with your name and all nine races, and send it to a Pargo pickup point of your choice at no extra cost.

Fair play

Run your own races and submit honest results. Running for someone else or submitting false or altered results means disqualification, without a refund.

Questions

Email support@energylabapp.co.za. If you disagree with a verification decision, tell us and we’ll review it.
